Which Carburetor Size Should You Choose? (Raptor 660 & Street/Track Guide)

Ditching the stock dual carbs eliminates synchronization headaches and sharpens throttle response—but matching the carburetor bore to your engine build and riding style is key:

Stage 1 · Stock & Trail / Street

39mm – 40mm (FCR 39, TM40, Dell'Orto 40)

Best all-around choice for stock to mildly piped engines. Ideal for Raptor 660 trail/woods riding or SZR/Skorpion street use where easy cold starts, crisp bottom-end snap, and strong low-to-midrange torque matter most.

Stage 2 · Tuned Trail, Dunes & Track

41mm – 42mm (FCR 41, TM42, Dell'Orto 41, Malossi 41, Lectron)

Built for bolt-on & internal upgrades. Ideal if your Raptor 660 or road bike runs a full exhaust, open intake/airbox mod, hotter cam, or higher-compression piston and wants stronger mid-to-top-end pull in the dunes or on track without losing trail manners.

Stage 3 · Drag, Dunes & Big-Bore Race Only

45mm – 48mm (TM45/48 & HSR45/48)

Not for stock engines. A 45–48mm bore lowers intake velocity on a stock 660, hurting bottom-end throttle response on tight trails. Choose this dedicated large-runner manifold only for big-bore kits, ported heads, aggressive cams, and high-RPM Raptor drag/hillshoot or Supermono race builds.
