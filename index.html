Which Carburetor & Manifold Should You Choose?

1. 39mm – 40mm (FCR 39, TM40, Dell'Orto 40)

Best for Stock & Mild Street Builds: Keeps intake air velocity high at low-to-mid RPM for easy starting, clean jetting, and crisp everyday street/canyon throttle response.

2. 41mm – 42mm (FCR 41, TM42, Dell'Orto 41, Malossi 41, Lectron)

Best for Tuned Street & Track-Day Builds: Ideal when paired with an open exhaust, airbox mod, performance cam, or higher compression for stronger mid-to-top-end pull.

3. 45mm – 48mm (TM45/48 & HSR45/48 — Race / Big-Bore)

Not for stock engines: Features a larger-bore runner start to match 45–48mm carburetors. Best reserved for ported heads, big-bore kits, and high-RPM Supermono race builds—oversized carbs on a stock motor will hurt low-end response.
